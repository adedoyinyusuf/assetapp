# Demo Story: Modernizing NPOPC Infrastructure

This demo showcases the full lifecycle of asset management within the NPOPC Asset Management System, covering Procurement, Maintenance, Disposal, and Reporting.

## Scenario
The National Population Commission is upgrading its infrastructure for the upcoming census. This involves acquiring new technology, maintaining existing field vehicles, and disposing of obsolete office equipment.

## Asset Classes Involved
1.  **IT Equipment**: High-performance Servers (New Acquisition)
2.  **Furniture**: Executive Office Chairs (New Acquisition)
3.  **Electronics**: Census Tablets (New Acquisition & Maintenance)
4.  **Vehicles**: Field Operations Hilux (Maintenance of Existing Asset)
5.  **Office Equipment**: Legacy Photocopiers (Disposal of Obsolete Asset)

## Demo Flow

### 1. Procurement (Acquiring New Assets)
*   **Action**: Create Procurement Requests for new infrastructure.
*   **Request 1 (IT)**: "Data Center Upgrade" - 2x Dell PowerEdge Servers.
*   **Request 2 (Furniture)**: "Executive Wing Refresh" - 5x Ergonomic Chairs.
*   **Request 3 (Electronics)**: "Census 2025 Pilot" - 50x Samsung Galaxy Tablets.
*   **Outcome**: Requests are logged and visible in the Procurement Dashboard.

### 2. Maintenance (Operational Upkeep)
*   **Action**: Report issues with assets to ensure operational continuity.
*   **Request 1 (Vehicle)**: Report a "Toyota Hilux" (Existing Asset) for critical brake maintenance.
*   **Request 2 (Electronics)**: Report one of the new "Samsung Galaxy Tablets" for a cracked screen during field testing.
*   **Outcome**: Work orders are generated, and asset status changes to "Under Maintenance".

### 3. Disposal (End of Life Management)
*   **Action**: Retire assets that are no longer economically viable.
*   **Request 1 (Office Equipment)**: Request disposal for a "Canon ImageRunner" photocopier.
*   **Reason**: Obsolete / Parts Unavailable.
*   **Outcome**: Asset is flagged for disposal and removed from active inventory counts.

### 4. Reporting & Analytics (Management Overview)
*   **Action**: Review the system-wide status.
*   **View**: Dashboard / Reports.
*   **Metrics**:
    *   Total Asset Value.
    *   Assets by Status (Active, Maintenance, Disposed).
    *   Recent Activity Log.
